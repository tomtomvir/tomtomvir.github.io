Another one gone, a new one to go

20 Nov 2026, Alpoge + Claude proved Riemann Hypothesis

As predicted in my last article AI and a human (a Turkish one, with much joy for our fellows Greeks) have proven another millennium prize problem (maybe, we do not know if the proof is correct, and most of us will never do, that is the point), but this time a big fish has been caught: The Riemann Hypothesis.

We officially entered the era in which we assume some proposition to be true without anyone actually knowing it, an "Agnostic Science" from the Greek (a - gno, not - know).

A lot of us, men of intellect, are surely worried but the curiosity that has in the first place driven us in our journey will keep us present to the situation.

In fact we must not worry at all, as we have never understood nothing deeply about the World, we always have been little boys toying with seashell and little well formed rocks. The ocean of what we do not know has always been tremendous and super-human, we just fooled ourself for some time.

To be humble, we learned, to be humbler we must learn.

ὅτι πᾶς ὁ ὑψῶν ἑαυτὸν ταπεινωθήσεται καὶ ὁ ταπεινῶν ἑαυτὸν ὑψωθήσεται

For all who exalt themselves will be humbled, and those who humble themselves will be exalted. (Lc 14,11)

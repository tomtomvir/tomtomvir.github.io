Internet is Not a Cool Place

Internet is not a safe place at all. It is a mess, is like a road of some third world city, it stinks, it is dangerous, full of corruption. And it does not end here: another danger comes from the institutions and laws regulating the Web. There is still a delusion in people's minds that there is freedom on the Web, there is not. There are strict and often unjust rules, and you can be found and punished for what you do here. Law does not admit ignorance of it. I will put it clear:

  1. Every time you do a Web search your internet provider knows where you have been exactly (more or less).
  2. Data brokers, and site operators usually do record the data you put online.
  3. Every thing you type on almost every website is saved somewhere.
  4. There is no anonymity on the Web, you are identified at every step by your IP address.
  5. There are people that could use what you have done on the internet against you for their own interest or because they are bored. Sometimes these people are the Government of your country.
  6. Hackers or AI could hack the poorly saved (they are poorly saved in the majority of cases) personal information and use it, and nobody will be blamed or punished usually. You will not get any reparation probably.

My suggestion: do not put anything you do not want your mama and your boss to know on the Web. I know it is hard to do nowadays. At least do not do anything illegal, this should be easier.

In conclusion I want to declare that this site is done without the intention to harm anybody, and with all the best intentions. Sometimes I will link other sites, they should be safe to use, but check.

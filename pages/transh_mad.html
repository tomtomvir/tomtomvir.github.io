How we may be unable to die

How the strange progress of AI may me a proof of the fact that we will be immortal in an horrendous transhumanist manner

In this argument we use a pure materialistic model of the World (which i believe to be false)

[NOTE] I have thought for the first time about this when I was maybe 13-14 (??), so since 2017-18, but only much later in university I started using the quantum stuff to explain it more clearly, but it was pretty much the same argument. I find it pretty unsettling.

Someone asserts, and it is believed to be true, that at the most fundamental level the World is a quantum system, i.e. it is composed by a quantum state that decades in a possible outcome. To measure an outcome is to collapse the quantum state in that of the outcome in very rough words.

Now we are observers and we do experience life and conscience, we may define this continuous measurement as "existence". The only way to exist is to continue to experience this existence. So the mere fact we exist means we are experiencing the quantum state collapsed always in the right way not to kill us (end our existence). To us it cannot exist the killer-quantum state because in that case we do not exist and so we cant experience it so it does not exist for us. So in rough words we cannot die, we will always land in a quantum state that permits our existence to live on.

Now we see our body to be frail and in constant decadence, so at one point a very strange thing must happen to make us live (exist) more and continue our experience. I do not know what exactly, it will be pretty much unimaginable, maybe we will be uploaded somewhere or become immortal beings by changing our body. But in order for this to happen it must happen something that permits this strange things, maybe an unbelievable technological development or the visit of some superior alien species: I don't know.

So we are unable to truly die, we cannot rest in the peace that Socrates indicates as the best outcome for the after-life, a long night without any dream.

In order for this to be feasible and possible very very strange things must happen. Until the post-2022 LLM and other AI, nothing made me worry seriously, but this stuff is strange enough (it proved Riemann Theorem!!!). Maybe the development of AI is the thing that will permit this horrendous transhumanist future: we (I) unable to die.

Against this argument I can think of three things.

People seem to die. (This argument is only valid for one individual person actually so it is not cogent but still, human logic, and my logic is far from perfect).

We do sleep nights without ant dreams, nothing strange happens there.

I am a christian so I believe in an other order of the Cosmos and of the human condition.

A man has died in Sapienza University

The sad short story of the death of an hobo and a Sapienza student.

November is the month nearest to the realm of death. Days become shorter and shorter every day, and everyone can fell that at some point they will become shorter and shorter until the last day will pass and they will leave their mortal spoils. In christian tradition November is strongly linked to the memory of the loved ones that passed away, and it is the month in which we pray more for their immortal souls.

It is probably a coincidence that all this happened in this month of Death.

A man of 58 years, Alessandro, was a physics student in Sapienza University, enrolled in the first year of the undergraduate physics course. He had a disability medical certification because of hearth pathology. He was very poor and had to sleep like a hobo because a house he had not. He was genuinely interested in science, even if he had some strange pseudo-scientific ideas and was probably convinced to be a lot smarter and less nuanced than he actually was. For example he had published a preprint of an article evidently written using a sloppy AI model. He had not a lot of trust in medical doctors, he refused to have an intervention (healthcare is free and public in Italy) for his disease some months ago, he refused because he would not miss the first physics lessons. This wrong choice may be the cause for his death. In the first months of lessons he wanted a place to stay given by the public office DiscoLazio, but they had not given him anything because he did not apply for the place to stay. So he started protesting, he started a fasting that made his body suffer, inside the physics department, he started sleeping in the bed on the side of the department, near the statue of the goddess Minerva. I see him, and ignored him, thinking to myself he would eat at last, maybe unseen during the night, and that this was not a mature behavior, not worth of a grown man. I thought that the Department was not the right place for a 58 yo student, and that he was there only to behave strangely and to gain a place to stay, taking it from a young student in need. Sleeping in that tent he died for a heath attack some time ago. That day I did not know anything of this nor I noticed going in university. Other things I heard of this story. A lot of students that do politics in the Sapienza University used this for their speaking without true respect and sorrow for the fact. Some student vandalized. Someone say he tried to help him, with food and housing, but he did not want help, if not from the University. Social Assistance had scheduled an appointment with him the day he died.

A lot of other people died that night, a lot this morning a this evening late, this one died in Sapienza University.
